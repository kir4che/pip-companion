Press Shift + Alt/Opt + P on a page with a video to toggle Picture-in-Picture (PiP).

Features
• PiP controls for playback, seeking (including timestamp entry), volume, speed, and frame stepping. Play next on supported sites.
• Playlists and thumbnail previews for YouTube, Bilibili, and Bahamut Anime Crazy.
• Show captions readable from the video in PiP, with customizable font, size, and color.
• Danmaku (scrolling comments): show native danmaku on Bilibili and Bahamut Anime Crazy, or display YouTube and Twitch chat as scrolling messages. Send from PiP on Bilibili, Bahamut Anime Crazy, and YouTube Live.
• Floating comment panels for YouTube and Bilibili.
• Save supported video frames as PNG.
• Follow playback: when enabled, switch to another playing video when the PiP video pauses.
• Resize PiP to fit the video's aspect ratio.
• In Settings, toggle danmaku, screenshots, floating comments, and follow playback; customize shortcuts, seek interval, caption and danmaku styles, and interface language (Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, or English).
• Detect videos in supported iframes; compatibility depends on the site and browser permissions.

Platform support and limitations: https://github.com/kir4che/pip-companion/blob/main/docs/platform-support.md

Privacy and support
• No personal data is collected for analytics. Sending danmaku connects to the relevant platform only when you use the feature.
• Report issues or suggest features: https://github.com/kir4che/pip-companion/issues
