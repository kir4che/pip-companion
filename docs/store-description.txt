Want to keep a video in view while you browse or work? Press Shift + Alt/Opt + P to open it in PiP.

What you can do
• Control video without switching back: play or pause, seek by dragging or entering a time, adjust volume and speed, or step frame by frame. Resize the PiP window to fit the video; play the next video on supported sites.
• Find what you want to watch: browse supported playlists, preview thumbnails on supported video timelines, or search danmaku for highlights.
• Subtitles your way: switch available subtitle tracks, customize how they look, or add a second subtitle line with live translation.
• See and join the conversation: on supported sites, show danmaku or turn live chat into danmaku. Search danmaku, filter keywords, and send messages from PiP where supported and permitted. You can also overlay danmaku from other sources on videos.
• Keep the discussion open: view floating comment panels for YouTube and Bilibili while you watch.
• Save a moment: capture video frames as PNG on supported platforms.
• Make PiP yours: reorder or hide playback controls, and customize shortcuts, subtitle styles, and danmaku appearance.
• When follow playback is enabled, the PiP switches to another playing video when its current video pauses.

Platform support and limitations: https://github.com/kir4che/pip-companion/blob/main/docs/platform-support.md

Privacy and support
• No personal data is collected for analytics. Sending danmaku or using subtitle translation connects to the relevant platform or service only when you use the feature.
• Report issues or suggest features: https://github.com/kir4che/pip-companion/issues
