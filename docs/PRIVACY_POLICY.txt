PiP Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

1. Data processing
The extension has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and the developer operates no server to collect user data. Content scripts run on regular webpages to find videos and process video, captions, chat/danmaku, and player state locally. Page content is not sent to the developer.

Shortcuts, feature toggles, and danmaku appearance settings are stored in the browser's local extension storage. The source-tab identifier is kept temporarily in session storage while PiP is in use. Screenshots are downloaded to the user's device when requested.

2. Third-party services
For Bilibili danmaku, the extension sends video identifiers and requested danmaku-segment information to the Bilibili API. Bilibili thumbnail previews also request thumbnail data from its API. YouTube thumbnail previews may request the video page using its video identifier and load YouTube thumbnails. Bahamut Anime Crazy previews read thumbnail information provided by the page and load the images. These requests go directly to the services or their image hosts and may include cookies or other request information, depending on the request and browser settings. Each service's privacy policy governs its handling and retention of request data.

The extension does not send data to the developer and contains no analytics or tracking.

3. Retention and deletion
Removing the extension or clearing its browser data deletes local settings. The source-tab identifier is cleared when the browser session ends. Users manage downloaded screenshots themselves. Each third-party service determines how long it retains its request data.

4. Policy and contact
Updates to this policy will be published here. For privacy questions, contact the project through GitHub Issues:
